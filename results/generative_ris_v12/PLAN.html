二、三个补充方向

方向实际比较证据边界
① 条件何时有资格否决对象冻结 Marigold/VAE 主干，训练小颜色分类模块；校准后允许支持、反驳或未知；空间规则检查所有同名参照物的一致性。比较硬否决与保守否决。颜色模块是学习的；空间部分目前是几何规则，使用标注参照物框。
② 有限预算下先检查什么颜色优先、位置优先、随机、全部检查和自适应启发式；预算 1/2；记录调用数，并对固定样本实测检查阶段延迟。自适应策略尚未训练，不宣称已有新策略。实测检查延迟排除共享生成前端及预览，不等于整体加速。
③ 纠错与误伤边界分别报告纠正、误伤、条件误伤率；在清晰、降分辨率、遮挡副本及参照物歧义分层下观察变化。保守纠错已有论文；本轮验证它在低成本生成式候选上的适用性。

GQA：512 张训练、64 张校准、128 张测试图像，颜色模块 3 个种子；3 个候选均由冻结生成式模型输出。GQA 原始标注提供对象框和场景图，因此这部分报告条件准确率和对象框定位，不报告像素掩码真值成绩。诊断问题由原始属性及关系确定性构造，属于独立派生实验，不冒充官方 GQA 问答基准。

查新边界与已有工作

目前所有方向都只是候选贡献。实例对比、像素监督、不确定性门控、预算搜索和保守纠错均有先例。潜在贡献应来自明确的生成式适配失效机制、受控消融及可复现的适用边界；不是宣称首次加入一个组件。

  • Vision Banana — Image generation models adapted to perception; broad theme, not a reproduction.
  • Ref-Diff: Zero-shot Referring Image Segmentation with Generative Models — Generative-model referring segmentation precedent; not evidence of novelty here.
  • Prompt-Driven Referring Image Segmentation with Instance Contrasting — Existing instance contrast and BCE/Dice; these components are not new. Search-indexed paper extract read; direct PDF returned403.
  • Contrastive Grouping With Transformer for Referring Image Segmentation — Prior contrastive grounding and pixel losses.
  • CroBIM-U: Uncertainty-Driven Referring Remote Sensing Image Segmentation — Prior uncertainty gated language fusion; differs from decoder-fit gated paired training loss, novelty remains unproven.
  • RefChess — Prior language decomposition and budgeted contextual proposal search. Adaptive checking alone is not new.
  • Counterfactual Segmentation Reasoning — Counterfactual hallucination and abstention already investigated.
  • GQA — Original color and relation scene-graph annotations for separate condition/box diagnostics.
  • CLEVR-Ref+ — Prior compositional diagnostics including false-premise empty segmentation; considered alternative dataset.
  • DeRecG: Mitigating Hallucination in Long Referring Expressions via Training-Free, Anchor-Preserved Visual Grounding — Already studies conservative anchor preservation, correction/harm transitions and cost. Direction3 is an adaptation/diagnosis, not firstproposal.
  • RESAnything — Attribute prompting and candidate/proposal evaluation already exist.
  • Improving Selective Visual Question Answering by Learning From Your Peers — Selective prediction/abstention precedent; no claim of new genericabstention.
